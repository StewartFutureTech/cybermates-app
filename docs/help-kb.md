# Cybermates help knowledge base
Used offline by the in-app Help assistant (src/help.js) and as context for the optional Grok route (api/_fn/help.js).
Format: each `## ` heading is one answer; the `keywords:` line boosts matching. Keep answers short and factual.

## What is Cybermates?
keywords: about app what is social network modes
Cybermates is a social networking app for adults (18+) with three modes: 🤝 Friends, 🎮 Gaming and ❤️ Dating. You only match with people who share something real — games, platforms, shows, interests. Friends is the default mode; Dating is optional and only shown to people who turn it on.

## How do I switch modes (Friends, Gaming, Dating)?
keywords: mode switch friends gaming dating enable turn on
On Discover, tap Friends, Gaming or Dating at the top. If a mode isn't enabled yet, tap "Enable it". You can also change "What are you here for?" in Profile.

## How does matching work?
keywords: match matching percent score shared compatible why
You only see people who share at least one game, platform or interest and whose distance range includes you (and yours includes them). The % blends answers to optional questions with how much you have in common. Dating also checks both people's gender and age preferences.

## How do I change my distance range?
keywords: distance range radius miles km location region global nearby
Profile → "Distance per mode". Each mode has its own range: radius (mi/km), same city, same state/region, same country, or global. Defaults: Friends same region, Gaming global, Dating 50 mi (80 km outside US/UK). Both people's ranges must include each other.

## What are starters? Why can't I message someone?
keywords: starter message limit daily free cannot send new conversation
Starting a NEW conversation uses a starter: everyone gets 5 free per day (15 with Cybermates+), and you can buy more in Shop. Replies in existing conversations are always free and unlimited. If someone blocked you, your message is refused and the starter is refunded.

## What is a Boost?
keywords: boost pin inbox top refund
A Boost pins your message to the top of the other person's inbox until they read it. Buy boosts in Shop; Cybermates+ includes 5 per month. If your boosted message is never delivered or read before it expires, or they block you or delete their account, the boost comes back automatically (up to 10 a day; any more are checked by support first). Boosts on messages they already read are not refunded.

## How do I cancel Cybermates+?
keywords: cancel subscription plus refund billing unsubscribe
Deleting the app does NOT cancel a subscription. Use "Cancel subscription" in Profile → Advanced settings (or the link in your Stripe receipt email). It ends at the end of the current period.

## Do my boosts and starters move to a new phone?
keywords: transfer new phone device move boosts starters lost balance
Your profile, friends and settings transfer with "Transfer to new device" (Profile → Advanced settings). Boosts, bought starters and voice minutes are tied to the device and do NOT carry over. Cybermates+ is tied to your account and is restored.

## How do I transfer my account to a new phone?
keywords: transfer device code send receive new phone
On the OLD phone: Profile → Advanced settings → "Send from this phone". On the NEW phone tap "Receive" and type the 6-digit code within 10 minutes. The old phone is wiped after the transfer.

## Are my chats saved?
keywords: chat saved history privacy burn delete screenshot
Chats have no fixed deletion time. Chat saving has three options both people agree on: "Keep forever", "Keep for a while, plus 7-day safety copy" and "Hide instantly, kept 7 days for safety reports". When you both pick the same option, this phone follows it for the visible chat history (forever = keep; for a while ≈ about 30 days on screen; hide instantly = hide from the chat view right away). Every option also keeps an encrypted 7-day safety copy of recent messages on this phone so you can report abuse; that copy is wiped after 7 days. Under-18 chats will always be kept, with no option. Messages to someone who is offline wait on your phone, encrypted, for up to 7 days. Burn mode removes messages after reading but is best-effort — the other person can still take screenshots.

## Who can see my profile?
keywords: privacy profile visible online stub who can see
Your full profile is visible only while you are online; when you go offline others see a small stub. Linked accounts (Discord, Steam, Instagram…) have per-account privacy: Everyone, After mutual match, or Hidden. Religion and orientation are optional, off by default, and never leave your phone.

## How do I block someone?
keywords: block stop contact harass
Open their profile or chat and tap Block. Their messages are dropped and they are told they were blocked.

## How do I report someone?
keywords: report abuse harassment scam fake underage threat illegal
Tap Report on any profile or chat, or use "Report a user" right here in Help. Choose a reason and optionally attach a short excerpt. Our safety team reviews reports; illegal-content reports are prioritised. If anyone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) first.

## Safety tips for meeting people
keywords: safety meet date public scam money tips background check
Cybermates does NOT run criminal background checks or identity verification. Meet in public, tell a friend where you're going, never send money or gift cards, and report anyone who asks. Some US states require dating-safety notices; you'll see them in Dating mode where they apply.

## How do I contact a human / the safety team?
keywords: human contact support email safety team escalate person staff
This Help chat is an AI assistant, not a human. To reach the safety & legal team, use "Contact safety team" below (email), or Report inside the app.

## What is the peer network / mesh, and the kill switch?
keywords: mesh peer network p2p consent kill switch shutdown disabled offline
Cybermates connects phones directly (peer-to-peer) after you consent. Cybermates can shut the peer network down for everyone, a user or a session at any time (kill switch); when that happens connections close immediately and a red banner explains why. You can withdraw consent in Profile.

## The app says offline / retrying
keywords: offline retrying loading jacking in syncing connect slow
When you open the app it joins the mesh ("jacking in"). If no peers answer within about 20 seconds you're let in anyway with an offline notice, and it keeps retrying in the background. Check your connection; nothing is lost.

## How does voice setup work?
keywords: voice setup microphone speech grok onboarding
Voice setup is optional and needs separate consent. Free voice uses your phone's speech recognition; audio is transcribed and discarded, never stored. Paid Grok voice is an AI (xAI) running under zero data retention. Typed setup is always free.

## How do I change the theme, glow or sound?
keywords: theme color dark light pink blue green glow sound mute look
Tap 🎨 in the header to cycle themes, or Profile → "Look & sound" to pick Neon Cyan, Synth Pink, Deep Blue, Acid Green or Light, toggle glow effects, and turn the boot sound on/off (off by default).

## Age requirement
keywords: age 18 minimum underage teen minor parent games cross-age
Dating and Roulette are 18+ only. Under-18 accounts (13–17) get friends, chat and games. Friends, Dating, Discover and Roulette are always kept separate by age: under-18 and 18+ accounts never see, match or friend each other there, because those age groups should not meet in person. In games (matchmaking, lobbies, spectating) under-18 and 18+ players can play together by default; only the game name and avatar are shown, and there is no chat, voice, DM or friend request across ages. A parent can turn cross-age games off in the under-18 account: 👪 Parent controls → "Games with 18+ players". If you believe someone lied about their age, report them with the reason "underage user".
