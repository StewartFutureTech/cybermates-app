Toward a peer that announced one, directed envelopes are v2: `{id, from, to, iv, ct, epk, pk}`, signed, epk/pk also in the AES-GCM AAD; key = SHA-256("cm-chat-v2" | pair | epk | pk | X25519(eph, their prekey) | X25519(my static, their static)). The sender ephemeral is per peer and rotates every 10 min or 256 messages; its secret is zeroed right after the key is derived. Prekey secrets are erased 8 days after a newer prekey replaced them (longer than store-and-forward and the 7-day server copy), so recorded traffic can't be opened later, not even from a seized phone with its long-term keys and vault. Receive keys are cached per (sender, epk, prekey) (bounded 256). Pre-v47 clients announce no prekey and keep getting v1 (static) envelopes, and their v1 envelopes still open. `src/app-mesh.js` keeps the prekeys in the encrypted vault (`meshIds[x].pre`) and saves every rotation.
  - **PAKE transfer (`src/pake.js`, `src/transfer.js`).** CPace-style over X25519 with no new dependency: generator = Elligator2(SHA-512(DSI | code | sid)) (BigInt field math), shares `Y = X25519(y, G)`, ISK = SHA-512(DSI | sid | Y_recv | Y_send | K) -> AES-GCM key + HMAC key confirmation both ways. Receiver-first: `x2-req {rid, sid, Y}` -> `x2-ans {Y, kc}` -> `x2-proof {kc}` -> `x2-data` -> `x2-ok`. A recording of the wire gives nothing to test codes against. The rendezvous is a 10-way first-digit bucket; before v47 it was SHA-256 of the full code, which alone revealed the code. The sender answers at most 5 shares (each is one online guess), then burns the code. Unauthenticated `x-err` can't abort a real transfer, and two transfers in the same bucket both work. `newCode()` now has no modulo bias.
  - **Tests.** `scripts/smoke-e2efs.mjs` (50 checks: PAKE, transfer, forward secrecy incl. seized phone + old-client compat, perf v2 ~ v1 since Ed25519 dominates) and `scripts/e2e-e2efs.mjs` (2 real tabs: v2-only chat + receipt, prekeys encrypted at rest and restored after reload, real Settings-UI transfer wrong/right code) are in regress.sh; smoke-secure / smoke-approved / smoke-idbind unchanged and passing.
  - **Still open.** (1) Hardware-backed key storage: needs a native secure-storage plugin (Keystore / Keychain) and a physical phone to verify. (2) Forward secrecy for the server-relayed Supabase chat path (`supabase-e2e.js` static nacl.box; dormant until `VITE_SUPABASE_*`) needs a prekey column/table + migration, so it's the next step once that transport is switched on. (3) A device linked by v37 shares the network ID but has its own box keys, so (as before v47) a directed message only opens on the device whose keys the sender last saw.
